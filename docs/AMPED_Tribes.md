# AMPED: Music Tribes
### Character & art bible for Metal, Punk, Pop, Electronic and Hip Hop bands

Each tribe is a playable band genre **and** a source of rival bands, crowds and venues.
All tribes share the same art style as The Static Kids so they live in one world.

---

## 0. Shared style rules (all tribes)

**Keep identical across every tribe**
- Full-body character sprite, front or 3/4 view, standing pose with their instrument
- Clean, thick dark outline; soft cel shading (2–3 tones per colour)
- Slightly chibi proportions (head ≈ 1/5.5 of height), big expressive eyes, readable silhouette
- Chunky footwear, baggy or layered clothing, one strong signature colour per character
- Transparent background, no text, no logos, centred, feet visible

**What changes per tribe:** silhouette shapes, palette, materials, accessories, attitude, poses.

**Base prompt (paste first, then add the character line)**
> 2D cartoon character sprite, full body, cozy anime-inspired style, clean thick dark outlines, soft cel shading, vibrant saturated colours, slightly chibi proportions, big expressive eyes, chunky shoes, standing pose holding instrument, isolated on transparent background, no text, game character art, consistent with a cozy indie music game.

**Turnaround/consistency:** for each character, generate a neutral pose first, lock it, then ask for poses (idle, play, cheer, tired) and outfit variants *using that image as reference*.

## 6. How tribes interact in the game

| | Metal | Punk | Pop | Electronic | Hip Hop | Indie (Static Kids) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fans love** | Skill, loudness | Energy, honesty | Hooks, visuals | The drop, the vibe | Lyrics, confidence | Songs, story |
| **Fans dislike** | Selling out | Expensive tickets | Sloppy shows | Breaks in the flow | Fakeness | Being ignored |
| **Best venues** | Cellars, open air | Squats, DIY spaces | Malls, TV, arenas | Warehouses, clubs | Block parties, radio | Pubs, clubs, festivals |
| **Rhythm style** | Fast, dense, solos | Short, chaotic | Beat + dance swipes | Build-up + drop | Flow + battle comebacks | Balanced, melodic |
| **Economy** | Loyal, slow growth | Cheap, low cash | Big streams, high cost | Night gigs pay double | Viral lyrics, local scene | Middle ground |

**Mixing tribes**
- Festivals can have a **genre mix**; playing outside your tribe is risky but can win new fans
- **Collabs:** two player bands of different tribes release a crossover single (bonus to both fan bases), e.g. Metal × Electronic, Hip Hop × Pop, Punk × Hip Hop
- **Crowd reactions** depend on the tribe mix in the audience; read the crowd and adjust the setlist

## 8. Characters & skins (built so skins can be sold later)

Skins only work if the character system is planned for them **from the first drawing**. Changing it later means redrawing everything.

### 8.1 Character system
| Type | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Story characters** | Fixed identity, appear in the story and as rivals | Moira, Rex Gold, Velvet Riot, Iron Hollow |
| **Band members** | Playable characters the player recruits into their band, about 8–12 per tribe at launch | Nia, Spud, Vex, Kai… |
| **Player identity (multiplayer)** | Each real player picks one band member as "their" character in a player band | You play as Theo in a friend's band |
| **Skins** | Alternate looks for a band member: outfit, hair and accessories, sometimes a special effect | Nia's 6 outfits from the outfit sheet |

**Recommendation:** no build-your-own character from parts at launch. That's very hard to do with painted art. Use a big roster of characters plus skins plus colour tints done in code. A part-based character creator can come much later, if ever.

### 8.2 Art rules every skin must follow
- A skin is a **complete set of images for one character**: `idle`, `play`, `cheer`, `tired` (+ `portrait` and a store `thumbnail`)
- **Same canvas size, same foot line, same head position** as the base character, so code can swap it with no jumps
- **Same face, hair shape and silhouette** so the character stays recognisable (change clothes, colours and accessories; never the face)
- **Instruments on their own layer** (separate transparent image). This lets instruments be **sold separately** and swapped on any skin. Decide this now.
- Files:
```
assets/characters/<characterId>/base/<pose>.webp
assets/characters/<characterId>/skins/<skinId>/<pose>.webp
assets/instruments/<instrumentId>.webp
```

**Skin data (example)**
```json
{
  "id": "nia_punk_leather",
  "character": "nia",
  "name": "Leather & Tartan",
  "tribe": "punk",
  "rarity": "rare",
  "price": { "coins": 0, "gems": 400 },
  "earnable": false,
  "poses": ["idle", "play", "cheer", "tired"],
  "effect": null,
  "season": "S1"
}
```

### 8.3 What can be sold
| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Character skins** | Main product. Tribe-themed outfits for every character |
| **Instrument skins** | Guitars, drum kits, keytars, mics: needs instruments drawn separately |
| **Stage skins** | Stage backdrops, light colours, pyro styles for gigs |
| **HQ decor** | Posters, sofas, plants, rugs, neon signs for the band's base |
| **Festival decor** | For promoters: stage themes, banners, flags, entrance arches |
| **Emotes / victory poses** | Cheer animations after a gig or battle win |
| **Season pass** | Free track + paid track of cosmetics over 8–10 weeks |
| **Bundles** | e.g. "Metal Night bundle": skins for a whole band in one tribe |

### 8.4 Rarity tiers
| Tier | How to get | What makes it special |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Earned by playing | New colours / simple outfit |
| Rare | Earned or bought | New outfit with accessories |
| Epic | Bought or season pass | Full themed outfit + matching instrument |
| Legendary | Bought, limited time | Special animation or glow effect, unique cheer pose |

Keep a good number of skins **earnable for free**. Players who can earn some are more likely to buy others.

### 8.5 Rules (protect players and the business)
- **Cosmetic only.** Skins never change stats, scores or chart results. It's a competitive game.
- **No paid random boxes.** Sell items directly, or show exactly what's inside. Paid loot boxes are restricted or banned in some countries (e.g. Belgium) and under scrutiny across the EU.
- **Try before you buy:** preview the skin animated on the HQ screen and on stage
- **Show it off:** skins are visible to others at festivals, battles, profiles and in crowds. Visibility is what sells skins.
- **Kids:** age gate + spending limits; no pressure timers aimed at children

### 8.6 Money and legal checklist
- **Store fees:** Apple/Google take 15–30% of in-app purchases; web purchases (e.g. Stripe) cost less but rules differ per store
- **Ownership on the server:** purchases are stored and verified server-side (e.g. Supabase table `player_items`), never trusted from the phone
- **EU VAT** on digital goods; clear refund policy
- **Art rights:** confirm the art tool's licence allows **commercial sale**. Purely AI-generated images may have weak copyright protection in some countries, so have an artist refine final store items to strengthen ownership.
- **No real brands:** no real guitar brand logos, clothing brands or band names on skins

### 8.7 Production cost per skin
| Tier | Images to make |
|---|---|
| Common / Rare | 4 poses + portrait + thumbnail = 6 images |
| Epic | Above + matching instrument skin |
| Legendary | Above + effect/animation work (code or Spine) |

**Start small:** launch with 1 base + 2 skins per Static Kids member (15 skins). Add tribes and skins each season.
