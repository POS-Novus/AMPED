# Music credits

## Instruments
The in-game band plays sampled instruments rendered from **GeneralUser GS v2.0.3** by S. Christian Collins
(https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser). Licence: free for private and commercial music creation and
for use in software (see `design/instruments/LICENSE.txt`). The author notes that the origin of a few
samples cannot be fully confirmed; no claims have been reported since 2000. Re-check before a commercial launch.

## Songs
All songs are generated by the game (`game/js/composer.js`) and played on those instruments.
There are no licensed recordings at the moment. Add any future ones to `design/music/sources.json` and list them here.
